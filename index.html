Ready-to-Use Supplementary Food (RUSF)
Fortified Complementary Food

EAT ONE SACHET PER DAY = 530 kcal

Complements the supplementation of moderate acute malnutrition in children 6 months and older.

Usage Instructions:

Do Not Add Water

Do Not Heat

Wash Hands & Knead

Squeeze & Eat

Feed Child

Supplement With Breastfeeding

Ingredients:

Blend of non-hydrogenated vegetable oil (contains one or more of the following: palm, soybean, canola), sugar, whey powder, soy flour, nonfat milk powder, peanuts, vitamin and mineral premix, fully hydrogenated vegetable oil (palm).

ALLERGENS : peanuts, milk, soy. May contain traces of gluten.

Exclusive Breastfeeding

It is strongly recommended to start breastfeeding immediately after birth, exclusively breastfeed during the first 6 months and continue until at least 24 months.

Use Under Medical Supervision

Consultation with a health worker is recommended for assessing child’s development needs.

Consume within 24 hours of opening

Best stored below 30 °C, under dry and hygienic conditions and away from direct sunlight.
Consume in addition to family food. After opening, keep the sachet tightly closed.
